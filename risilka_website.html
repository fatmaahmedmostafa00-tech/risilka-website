An Egyptian early-stage initiative

Turning agricultural waste into industrial value.

Risilka is developing a pathway to transform agricultural residues into valuable materials for industry, beginning with the exploration of precipitated silica from rice husk.

Who we are

Risilka is an early-stage Egyptian initiative working at the intersection of green technology, circular economy and advanced materials. We aim to create value from locally available agricultural by-products through resource-efficient material development.

The challenge

Agricultural residues can be underutilized, while many industrial applications rely on specialized raw materials. This creates an opportunity to investigate how locally available biomass can become a useful industrial resource.

Our approach

1. Resource recovery

Explore agricultural residues, starting with rice husk, as a potential source of useful material components.

2. Material development

Investigate the extraction and development of precipitated silica through laboratory-scale work and technical validation.

3. Industrial relevance

Study potential applications, including rubber compounds and tire manufacturing, subject to testing and performance validation.

Our intended impact

Circular economy

Support the productive use of agricultural by-products.

Local value creation

Explore locally sourced alternatives for selected industrial material needs.

Resource efficiency

Contribute to reducing waste through material recovery and innovation.

Development stage

Risilka is at the pre-seed stage, focused on technical development, laboratory experimentation, and validating the feasibility and potential applications of its material.

Important: The project is under development. Product performance, specifications, commercial readiness and industrial suitability remain subject to testing and independent validation.

Collaboration

We welcome conversations with research institutions, laboratories, industrial partners, accelerators and investors interested in circular materials, agricultural waste valorization and sustainable manufacturing.
